We are looking for **PhD students** in AI. Our research asks how AI systems can remain reliable when assumptions break. The [research themes](/#research) on the home page give an overview of what we work on.
{: .text-justify}

##### How to apply

There are two routes, and you can use either:

- **ELLIS PhD Program:** apply at [apply.ellis.eu](https://apply.ellis.eu) by **October 31, 2026** and select Aleksandar Bojchevski as a potential advisor.
- **Directly:** send a single PDF (no external links) to Aleksandar Bojchevski via {% al_email_protect_link site.data.socials.email %} with the subject line `[PhD]`, including your CV, transcripts of records, the abstract of your Master's thesis, and a list of publications if available. Direct applications are considered as they arrive, until the positions are filled.

##### Your profile

- A Master's (or equivalent) degree in computer science or a closely related field
- A solid mathematical foundation (probability, statistics, linear algebra)
- Experience with machine learning and Python/PyTorch is a plus

##### What we offer

Fully funded positions with full benefits, paid according to the collective agreement TV-L, pay group E13 (100%). The starting date is flexible.
{: .text-justify}

We welcome applications regardless of gender, nationality, ethnic and social origin, religion/belief, disability, age, and sexual orientation. Don't hesitate to get in touch if you have any questions.
{: .text-justify}
